Turn 1 · design check + proposal

Design check

What happens today (1a)
01A tick is a colour swap. White box → mint box, instantly. No motion, no sound, no beat. The biggest "I did it" moment in the app gets less feedback than hovering a button. 02The checkbox ignores the app's tactile language. Every button lifts on hover and presses in on click; the 26px check box does neither, and its hit area is just the box. 03Finishing a day is a footnote. "All done!" is 12px mono in the corner. 04Done looks like empty. Tab counts and the toolbar badge just vanish at zero, so a finished Thursday reads the same as a blank one. 05No sense of progress. "3 left" counts down but never shows how far you've come. 06Clearing is abrupt. Ticked rows disappear in one frame.
What 1b does
Tick: box presses in and pops, the check springs in, ink-edged confetti bursts from the box, a strike draws across, the row flashes mint. Sound ladder: each tick plays one note higher than the last. Meter: one chunky cell per item, filling with a bounce. "3 of 7". Day done: a lemon stamp drops in, confetti falls, a short arpeggio plays. Zero earns a ✓ on the day tab and the toolbar badge. Untick stays quiet. Rewards only go one way. Clear sweeps rows out one by one; add drops the new row in. Timetable rows tick when you click anywhere on them. Reduced-motion users keep colour, strike and sound; particles are skipped.
